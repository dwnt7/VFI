Emergency Towing Services & Roadside Assistance

Henry’s Wrecker Service has the largest fleet of tow trucks and roadside assistance vehicles, covering the metro Washington, DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland areas. With convenient locations in Falls Church, Vienna, and Alexandria, VA, as well as Gaithersburg and Silver Spring, MD, our towing company responds to roadside emergencies quickly. If you need a tow truck right now, we have experienced professionals standing by.

LIGHT DUTY TOWING & RECOVERY

We routinely send tow truck operators to retrieve passenger motor vehicles. Once pulled out of roadside ditches, our towing operators transport cars to your chosen facility.

MEDIUM DUTY TOWING & RECOVERY

Backed by a diverse fleet, our operators are ready to perform towing services for Class 6 or 7 motor vehicles. We have powerful winches and tow truck assets that get the job done.

HEAVY DUTY TOWING & RECOVERY

Henry’s Wrecker Service is the go-to resource in Falls Church, Vienna, and Alexandria, VA, and Gaithersburg and Silver Spring, MD, for big rigs, construction equipment, or farming machinery

ROADSIDE ASSISTANCE

Flat tire changes, dead battery jumpstarts, vehicle lockout services, and gas and diesel fuel delivery are always available in roadside emergencies.

ACCIDENT RECOVERY SERVICES

We see our fair share of accidents in Northern Virginia, Maryland, and the Greater DC area. That experience is one reason we’re the primary accident recovery services provider.

Off-Road Recovery

Law enforcement, owner-operators, freight carriers, passenger vehicle drivers, contractors, and agricultural operations rely on Henry’s Wrecker Service for emergency off-road recovery.

Flatbed Towing

Flatbed towing services are available in every community we cover. We can send a flatbed truck for any car, truck, SUV, or commercial motor vehicle.

Long-Distance Towing

Consider cost-effective long-distance towing if you are moving to another area. Our services save community members time, reduce stress, and ensure your automobile arrives on time.

Concierge & Exotic Vehicle Towing

Our concierge & exotic towing service ensures your luxury, classic, or exotic car is transported with precision and the utmost care—on time and damage-free, every time.

HEAVY EQUIPMENT TRANSPORT

If you have a profit-driving asset that cannot be driven on Maryland or Virginia roads, Henry’s Wrecker Service can transport your heavy equipment from Point A to Point B.

MOBILE CRANE & ROTATOR SERVICE

Our mobile crane and rotator services save local businesses time and money. Our agile fleet can set up in tight quarters and raise materials and equipment to heights.

Private Property Impound

We remove unauthorized or abandoned vehicles quickly and legally.. Count on us to keep your property clear and compliant 24/7.

NON-HAZMAT CLEAN-UP RESPONSE

Our non-hazmat clean-up response team is ready to tackle spills, debris, and roadway obstructions quickly and efficiently.

Your Local Towing Service & Trusted Towing Company

After more than 60 years of dedicated service, we are proud and humbled to be your trusted towing company. Over the decades, we have built a reputation for reliability, efficiency, and professionalism, ensuring that drivers in the community always have a dependable option when they need assistance.

As a locally operated company, we take pride in serving our neighbors with the highest level of care. Our team understands the challenges of vehicle breakdowns and unexpected roadside issues, and we work hard to make the process as smooth as possible. We are equipped to assist with any situation, and we have a fleet ready to handle everything from light-duty towing to heavy-duty recoveries.

Why Choose Henry’s Wrecker Service?

If you break down late at night or in an unfamiliar place, Henry’s Wrecker Service has dedicated towing and roadside assistance experts ready to respond immediately. Vehicle troubles can happen anytime, so we operate around the clock to provide fast and reliable assistance.

With an extensive fleet of 150+ tow trucks and experienced operators, we ensure that help is just a phone call away, no matter the hour. Our team prioritizes quick response times and professional service, so you can feel confident that you are in good hands.

We bring more than 60 years of expertise and a strong commitment to customer satisfaction. Our operators can handle towing and roadside situations, from standard tows to complex recoveries. Whether you need assistance with a flat tire, a jump-start, or a heavy-duty tow, we are prepared to provide the right solution.
